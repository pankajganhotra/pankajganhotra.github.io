Who this covers

This policy covers Kafila Run, a 3-lane endless runner game for Android, published on Google Play under the package name com.kafilarun.game. "We" means the developer of Kafila Run.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal or device information. Kafila Run does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers or any other data, and it requests no Android permissions that could access them.

Information stored on your device

The game saves one number: your highest score, so it is still there the next time you play. It is kept in the app's private storage on your phone and is never sent anywhere.

You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings, or by uninstalling the game.

Network, ads and third parties

Kafila Run works entirely offline. It contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting software, and no third-party code that collects data. Because the app sends nothing, there is nothing for us to share with or sell to anyone.

Google Play itself handles downloads, updates and any reviews you post, under Google's Privacy Policy. We do not receive personal information about you from that process.

Children

The game collects no information from anyone, including children under 13. No personal information is gathered at any age.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game changes how it handles data, for example by adding online features, we will update this page and its effective date before that version is released. The updated policy will also be linked from the app's Google Play listing.

Contact

For questions about this policy, use the developer email address shown in the "App support" section of Kafila Run's Google Play listing.
